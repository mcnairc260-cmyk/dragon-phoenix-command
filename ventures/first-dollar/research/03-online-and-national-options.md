# 03 — Online and National Options (A–F)

Researched 2026-11-10. Read-only web search. No one was contacted. Nothing was bought.

**Method limit (read this first).** Page fetching was blocked in this session. Every figure below comes from web-search result extracts, not from opening the page. So **[VERIFIED]** here means "the search result attributed this figure to this official or primary URL". Re-open the link before quoting any number in public. Other tags: **[VENDOR CLAIM]** (blog or company with something to sell), **[ANECDOTAL]** (forum posts), **[ESTIMATE]** (my judgement). Sources are listed as [S#] at the end.

## Summary verdict

- **Only one online option fits "first $500 in weeks": a fixed-price AI service sold direct (C).** One to three sales reach $500. Cost to start is near zero. [ESTIMATE]
- **Marketplaces (D) have real AI demand but a shrinking, crowded front door.** Upwork AI-skilled work grew 55%, while active clients fell 10% [S1]. Use D as a side channel, not the plan. [ESTIMATE]
- **Products with no audience (A, B, E) are slow and long-tailed.** Medians are tiny and AI has flooded supply. Three polished unlaunched apps prove the problem is selling, not building. [ESTIMATE]
- **Permit-lead or bid-alert subscriptions (F) are the best recurring-revenue idea,** but they need direct sales to contractors. Treat as a month-2 follow-on. [ESTIMATE]
- **Nothing online beats talking to buyers.** Every fast path here still needs outreach. Online only changes who you contact. [ESTIMATE]

## A. Micro-SaaS

- **Base rates.** One dataset of 3,787 revenue-reporting indie SaaS shows a 2025-launch median of $168 MRR, with 23.5% above $1k MRR [VENDOR CLAIM] [S2]. It is self-selected: only products that report revenue. Real odds are lower. An Indie Hackers post estimated about 9 months on average to $1k MRR [ANECDOTAL] [S3]. MicroConf survey figures for time to first revenue: not found.
- **AI pressure.** Apple's App Store took in about 560,000 new apps in the first half of 2026, close to all of 2025, while downloads rose about 2% [VERIFIED, press reports] [S4].
- **Costs.** Stripe 2.9% + 30¢ per US card charge [VERIFIED] [S5].
- **Fit.** Building is easy for this founder. Distribution is the gap. Ongoing support load is high. Poor fit for a 30-day goal.

## B. Digital products (Gumroad, Etsy, templates)

- **Base rates.** One analysis of 146,271 Gumroad products puts the median creator at $72/month and under 5% above $1,000/month [VENDOR CLAIM] [S6]. Another says the median selling product grosses about $88 in its lifetime [VENDOR CLAIM] [S7]. Etsy has 5.6M active sellers [VERIFIED via secondary report of Etsy's 10-K] [S8]. Share of Etsy shops with sales and median income: not found. The "65% never sell" figure online is unsourced.
- **Fees.** Gumroad 10% + 50¢ direct, 30% via Discover [VERIFIED] [S9]. Etsy $0.20 per listing, 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 processing, and a possible one-time set-up fee ($15 when introduced) [VERIFIED] [S10].
- **AI pressure.** Press in 2026 covers AI-made listings flooding Etsy digital downloads [VERIFIED headline only] [S11]. Etsy reportedly tightened AI rules in October 2026, with shop removal possible [VENDOR CLAIM] [S12].
- **Fit.** Low admin. Almost no sales effort. But near-zero traffic without an audience. Poor odds in 30 days.

## C. Productized AI services sold online

- **Demand.** Upwork's 2026 skills report: AI integration work up 178%, AI chatbot development up 71%, AI-named skills up 109% versus 23% for others [VERIFIED] [S13]. About 45% of consumers used AI tools to find local businesses, up from about 6% a year earlier (BrightLocal) [VERIFIED via secondary reports] [S14]. SOCi reports ChatGPT recommends 1.2% of local business locations [VENDOR CLAIM] [S15].
- **Prices seen.** AI visibility (GEO/AEO) audits: a $197 fixed audit and a "converged" tier near $999 for a 48-hour fixed scope [VENDOR CLAIM] [S16]. Fixed-price automation builds at one agency: $5,000–$15,000 [VENDOR CLAIM] [S17]. Fiverr's own guide: about $341 average for custom GPT apps and $295 for AI agent work [VERIFIED] [S18].
- **Time to first client.** Reports range from one week to 2.5 months [ANECDOTAL] [S19]. Cold email for AI-agency offers is described as oversaturated, with reply rates near 3% [VENDOR CLAIM] [S19]. Aggregate base rate: not found.
- **Costs.** Stripe fees only [S5].
- **Fit.** Fixed scope means low admin and no scope creep. Delivery is mostly automatable with AI. Sales effort is the real cost: you must contact buyers. Narrow niches beat generic "AI automation".

## D. Freelance marketplaces

- **Demand.** Upwork Q3 2026: active clients 717,000, down 10%. GSV from AI-skilled freelancers up 55% to a run rate near $450M [VERIFIED] [S1]. Fiverr Q3 2026: revenue down 15.8%, active buyers down to about 2.4M, spend per buyer up to $373 [VERIFIED via secondary report] [S20]. Upwork job volume for n8n fell 14.5% in April 2026; average automation rate $39.46/hour [VENDOR CLAIM] [S21].
- **Fees.** Upwork fee 0–15% per contract; Connects $0.15 each; 10 free per month on Basic [VERIFIED] [S22]. Fiverr takes 20% [VENDOR CLAIM] [S23]. Contra charges freelancers no commission [VENDOR CLAIM] [S24].
- **Competition.** Typical bid costs about 12 Connects. Market reply rate was 5.1% in Q2 2026 (agency data) [VENDOR CLAIM] [S25]. So $100 buys about 55 bids and about 3 replies [ESTIMATE]. One freelancer reported 102 proposals with no real client [ANECDOTAL] [S25]. First client often takes 2–8 weeks [VENDOR CLAIM] [S26].
- **Fit.** Rejection-heavy, daily admin. Identity-verification suspensions are reported and are permanent [ANECDOTAL] [S27]. Could eat the whole $100.

## E. Paid newsletter / faceless YouTube

- **Base rates.** beehiiv: median free-to-paid conversion 0.62%; median 66 days to a first dollar [VERIFIED via secondary reports of beehiiv data] [S28]. So $500/month at $10 needs about 8,000 free readers [ESTIMATE]. YouTube needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours for ads [VENDOR CLAIM] [S29]. vidIQ: only 30.8% of sub-1,000 channels that added 52+ videos reached 1,000 subscribers within 360 days [VENDOR CLAIM] [S30].
- **AI pressure.** YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule (July 2025) led to channel terminations in January 2026 [VENDOR CLAIM] [S31].
- **Fit.** Slowest path. Worth doing for brand reasons, not for first dollars.

## F. Other: public-data subscriptions

- **Kept: permit leads for contractors.** List prices: $99–$499/month, $149/month flat, and $299–$999/month for NYC [VENDOR CLAIM] [S32]. Raw permit data costs about $3 per 1,000 records [VENDOR CLAIM] [S33]. Seller-side success data: not found. Many competitors already exist.
- **Kept, weaker: government bid alerts.** Small-business tools cluster at $39–$99/month; SAM.gov is free [VENDOR CLAIM] [S34].
- **Dropped: niche job boards.** Evidence: not found.
- **Fit.** Recurring revenue and highly automatable. But contractors must be sold by phone or in person. This overlaps with local offers.

## Ranked view [ESTIMATE]

**(i) Chance of a first $500 within 30 days**

1. **C — about 15–25%.** One sale can do it. Zero cost. Depends on making 50+ contacts.
2. **D — about 5–10%.** Demand is real, but replies are near 5% and Connects burn cash.
3. **F — about 5%.** Needs a working feed plus 3–5 paying contractors.
4. **B — under 3%.** Median product earns about $88 ever.
5. **A — under 3%.** Typical path is months.
6. **E — under 1%.** Median first dollar is day 66.

**(ii) Path to $5,000/month within 12 months**

1. **C** — two to five projects a month, or retainers. Fewest customers needed.
2. **F** — 20–50 subscribers at $99–$249. Sticky, but slow to sell.
3. **D** — works once reviews exist; best as a feeder for C.
4. **A** — possible, but only about a quarter of reporting products pass $1k MRR.
5. **B** — needs a hit or an audience.
6. **E** — needs thousands of readers first.

These are judgement calls, not measured odds. No source gives base rates for this exact founder profile.
