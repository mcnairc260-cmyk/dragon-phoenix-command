A 3-minute start ritual

Light the fire before you start the work.

Your breath is the controller. The camera reads your breathing and your pulse. No wearable. No buttons to press during the session. When your real breath follows the slow pace, the fire grows.

Before the camera turns on
  • Camera. It is on only while a session runs. The light at the top of this window shows its state at all times. One button stops it.
  • Video. Camera frames go to the Presage SmartSpectra SDK on this computer. Presage states that the SDK computes the readings on the device. This app does not record, save or send video.
  • Readings. Breathing rate, pulse and heart rate variability (HRV) show live on screen. They are saved only if you choose to save them at the end, only on this computer, and you can delete them.
  • Presage servers. The SDK connects to Presage to check the API key, register this device, check account usage and download its models. SDK telemetry is a separate report about the device and the session. It is OFF unless you turn it on below.
  • Not medical. For general wellness and information only. Not cleared by the FDA. Not for diagnosis or treatment. This app gives no health advice.
  • Only you. Use it on yourself. Do not point it at another person without their clear consent.
