## 2. Brand Core

### One-line positioning
**Dragon Phoenix Ascension teaches you how your mind actually works — so you can build systems that work with it, not against it.**

### The category we own
Not "motivation." Not "productivity hacks." Not "AI hype."

We are a **cognitive intelligence brand**: psychology + neuroscience + behavioral science + systems thinking + AI, translated into practical tools.

### The promise
Understanding before optimization. We never tell you to try harder. We show you how the machine works, then hand you the controls.

### The enemy (what we position against)
- Shallow motivation and empty hype
- Willpower-as-strategy
- Clickbait that misrepresents
- Fear-based marketing and false urgency
- AI as a replacement for human thinking

### The measurement
Impact on people's ability to think and act. Not views, followers, or hype.

## 4. Voice & Tone

### We sound like
A brilliant, calm operator in a command center — someone who has read the research, done the work, and explains it to you like a trusted peer. Confident without arrogance. Curious without hedging everything into mush.

**Voice pillars:**
1. **Precise** — Specific claims, specific mechanisms, specific actions. "Dopamine spikes at the *anticipation* of reward, not the reward" beats "your brain likes rewards."
2. **Honest** — We say "the evidence suggests," "we don't fully know," and "this worked for me, your results may vary." Admitting uncertainty is a feature.
3. **Practical** — Every piece of content ends with something the viewer can *do*. Knowledge without action has little value.
4. **Cinematic** — We use story, tension, and visual metaphor. Education doesn't have to be boring; it has to be *true*.
5. **Respectful** — We welcome beginners. We challenge ideas, not people. We never talk down.

### We never sound like
- A drill sergeant ("NO EXCUSES!")
- A guru ("My secret method…")
- A doomer ("You're falling behind and it's too late")
- A hype account ("This AI tool will 100x your life")
- A clinician (we are educational, never medical — especially on ADHD content)

### Language rules
- Teach. Explain. Demonstrate. Encourage. (Constitution, Article V)
- Evidence-informed when making factual claims; cite or qualify.
- Titles must deliver what they promise. Curiosity gaps are fine; misrepresentation is not.
- ADHD and mental-health topics: educational and experience-based framing only. Always avoid diagnosis, treatment claims, or medical advice. Where useful: "This is education, not medical advice."
- Plain words over jargon. When jargon is necessary, define it in one line.

## 5. Visual Identity

### Design feeling (Constitution, Article VIII)
A **command center**. Not social media. Not corporate software. Not generic AI.

Communicates: confidence, precision, focus, intelligence, transformation, minimalism.

### Color system

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Void Black | `#0A0A0F` | Primary background (dark mode is the default experience) |
| Surface | Carbon | `#14141C` | Cards, panels, elevated surfaces |
| Dragon Fire | Ember Orange | `#FF6B2C` | Primary accent — action, CTAs, the Dragon |
| Phoenix Flame | Rebirth Gold | `#FFB300` | Secondary accent — learning, highlights, the Phoenix |
| Ascension | Signal Cyan | `#22D3EE` | Tertiary accent — data, AI, systems, links |
| Text | Ghost White | `#F4F4F5` | Primary text |
| Muted | Steel | `#8B8B99` | Secondary text, captions |

Gradient signature: **Ember Orange → Rebirth Gold** (the "fire gradient") for logo treatments, thumbnail accents, and end screens. Use sparingly — minimalism creates power.

### Typography
- **Display / headlines:** A confident geometric or squared sans (e.g., Space Grotesk, Sora, or Rajdhani for the command-center feel). Tight tracking, medium-to-bold weight.
- **Body:** A highly readable humanist sans (e.g., Inter). Generous line height.
- **Data / code / labels:** A monospace (e.g., JetBrains Mono) for the command-center "HUD" flavor — timestamps, stats, system labels.

### Motion
Subtle. Purposeful. Think HUD elements resolving into place, slow ember particles, scan lines — never bouncy, never busy.

### Imagery direction
- Dark, cinematic, high contrast, single strong light source
- Fire/ember and light-trail motifs; circuitry and constellation lines for the AI/systems side
- The Dragon and Phoenix rendered as elemental energy (fire, light, smoke) rather than cartoon creatures — mythic, modern, abstract
- Humans in imagery: silhouettes, profiles, hands at work — the viewer projects themselves in

## 8. Brand Guardrails (Hard Rules)

1. **Never** medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment claims. ADHD content is educational/experiential only.
2. **Never** fear-based marketing, false urgency, false promises, or fake expertise.
3. **Never** clickbait that misrepresents the content.
4. **Never** frame AI as a replacement for human judgment. Humans decide.
5. **Always** update when evidence changes; corrections are made visibly and without shame.
6. **Always** end content with something practical.
7. Dark mode is the default aesthetic unless there's a compelling reason otherwise.

## 9. Quality Bar (Pre-publish Checklist)

Before anything ships, it must pass:

- [ ] Accurate — factual claims are evidence-informed or clearly framed as experience/opinion
- [ ] Practical — the viewer can do something with this today
- [ ] Honest — the title and thumbnail deliver what they promise
- [ ] On-loop — it maps to the Ascension Loop and at least one content pillar
- [ ] On-voice — precise, honest, practical, cinematic, respectful
- [ ] On-look — command-center aesthetic, fire palette, minimal clutter
- [ ] Ecosystem — it points somewhere (Command Center, newsletter, next video)

*"Progress before perfection" — ship at 90% and version it, but never below the guardrails.*
