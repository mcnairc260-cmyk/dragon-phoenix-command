# Princess Lia and the Littlest Knight

*A tale of two brave hearts — starring Lia (4) and Lachlan (2).*

**Format:** picture book, ages 2–4, cover + dedication + 14 story pages.
**Art style (locked for every image):** warm 3D animated storybook style, Pixar/Disney-like, big expressive blue eyes, soft glowing painterly light, rich saturated color, gentle rounded shapes, magical golden-hour atmosphere.

**Character sheets (locked for every image):**
- **Princess Lia** — 4-year-old girl, golden-blonde softly wavy shoulder-length hair, big bright blue eyes, rosy cheeks, warm little smile. Wears a rose-pink and gold princess gown with a small gold tiara.
- **Sir Lachlan** — 2-year-old boy, short sandy-blonde hair, deep blue eyes, round chubby cheeks. Wears a tiny royal-blue knight tunic with a gold star on the chest, a slightly-too-big silver helmet tipped to one side, and carries a small wooden toy sword.
- **Ember** — baby dragon, small and round as a puppy, soft coral-pink and gold scales, huge teary amber eyes, tiny wings, utterly unscary and adorable.

**Illustration:** none (decorative star motif).

## Page 1

Once upon a time, in the golden Kingdom of Everbright, there lived a little princess named Lia. She had sunshine hair, sky-blue eyes, and the kindest heart in all the land.

**Illustration:** Lia in her pink gown twirling in a castle garden full of pink roses and butterflies, golden castle towers behind her.

## Page 2

And wherever Princess Lia went, there toddled her favorite knight in the whole wide world — Sir Lachlan the Littlest. His sword was wooden. His helmet was wobbly. But his heart was as big as the castle. "Go-go!" he cheered. It was his favorite word.

**Illustration:** Lachlan marching proudly across the castle courtyard, helmet slipping over one eye, wooden sword raised high; Lia giggling behind him.

## Page 3

Every night, the Great Star above the castle filled Everbright with light and color. But one morning, Lia looked out her window and gasped. The roses were turning gray! The banners drooped! High above the tallest tower, the Great Star had gone dim.

**Illustration:** Lia at an arched castle window looking worried; outside, the kingdom fading to gray, a pale flickering star above the tower.

## Page 4

"Someone has to bring back the light," said Princess Lia, putting on her bravest face. Sir Lachlan lifted his little wooden sword. "Go-go!" And so, hand in hand, the two marched out through the great castle gates.

**Illustration:** The two walking through enormous open castle gates onto a winding path, Lia leading, Lachlan's sword held high, morning light ahead.

## Page 5

They tramped into the Whispering Woods, where the trees hummed lullabies and fireflies lit the path like tiny floating lanterns. "Hello, trees!" called Lia. The leaves waved back.

**Illustration:** Magical glowing forest, giant friendly trees, hundreds of golden fireflies, the two small figures on a mossy path looking up in wonder.

## Page 6

At the Giggling Brook, they hopped from stone to stone. Hop! Hop! Whoops — Lachlan slipped! But quick as a wish, Lia caught his hand. "I've got you, little knight," she said. "We stick together."

**Illustration:** Sparkling brook with stepping stones, Lia holding Lachlan's hand mid-wobble, water splashing playfully, laughing fish peeking out.

## Page 7

Deep in the woods, a silver owl swooped down. "Whoo seeks the light?" she asked. "We do!" said Lia. The owl blinked her moon-round eyes. "The Great Star fell from the sky… into the Dragon's Cave on Moonberry Hill."

**Illustration:** Elegant silver owl on a low branch talking to the children, moonberry bushes with glowing purple berries around them.

## Page 8

A dragon?! Lia's tummy did a flip-flop. But she squeezed Lachlan's hand, and Lachlan squeezed right back. That is how brave works — it's easier when you hold hands. Up, up Moonberry Hill they climbed, all the way to the deep, dark cave.

**Illustration:** The two climbing a purple-flowered hillside at dusk toward a cave mouth with a warm golden glow inside, holding hands, determined little faces.

## Page 9

But inside the cave there was no big scary dragon at all. There was a *baby* dragon — small and round as a puppy — curled around the glowing Great Star. And the baby dragon was… crying. Plip. Plip. Plip.

**Illustration:** Cozy cave interior lit by the radiant golden star; tiny pink-and-gold baby dragon hugging it, big teary amber eyes, sparkly tears.

## Page 10

"I'm sorry," sniffled the little dragon. "My name is Ember. I was cold, and I was lonely, and the star was so warm… I didn't know the whole kingdom would go gray."

**Illustration:** Close-up of Ember looking up apologetically at the children, one tear rolling; Lia kneeling to listen, Lachlan peeking from behind her.

## Page 11

Princess Lia did not draw a sword. Princesses like Lia know a better magic. She wrapped Ember in the biggest, warmest hug. "You don't have to be lonely anymore," she said. "Come home with us." Lachlan patted Ember's nose. "Go-go!"

**Illustration:** Lia hugging the baby dragon tight, Lachlan patting its snout, the star's golden light wrapping all three in a warm glow.

## Page 12

So Ember stretched her little wings — flap, flap, WHOOSH! — and flew Lia and Lachlan up, up, past the clouds, to hang the Great Star back in the sky. It burst into light! And every color came flooding home: pink for the roses, purple for the banners, gold for every tower.

**Illustration:** The three flying on the baby dragon above the kingdom at night, placing the blazing star in the sky, waves of color spreading across the land below.

## Page 13

That night, the castle held the grandest ball Everbright had ever seen — for Princess Lia, Sir Lachlan the Littlest, and Ember, the newest friend of the kingdom. Because the bravest thing of all isn't a sword or a crown. It's a kind heart.

**Illustration:** Joyful castle ballroom, Lia twirling in her gown, Lachlan dancing with his helmet finally straight, Ember wearing a tiny bow, confetti and candlelight.

## Page 14

And when the music grew soft and the candles grew sleepy, the littlest knight climbed onto the princess's shoulder and closed his eyes. High above the castle, the Great Star shone its brightest — right over two brave hearts. Goodnight, Lia. Goodnight, Lachlan. The end.

**Illustration:** Quiet balcony at night; Lia sitting with sleeping Lachlan against her shoulder, Ember curled at their feet, huge warm star above the castle.
