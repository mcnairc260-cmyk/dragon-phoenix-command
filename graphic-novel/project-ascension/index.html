CHAPTER 2
THE SEED
Jonah Reyes used to keep the government's secrets.
Now he keeps a list.
Every name on it is dead, missing, or lying.
Tonight, the list wrote back.
UNKNOWN: You read my file.
Come read the rest.
— 9
Under the city, someone has been keeping
a different kind of archive.
SILASYou're the archivist.
REYESI was.
SILASThen you know my name.
Subject Nine.
Some people died.
He didn't. The file says otherwise.
They called it the Ascension serum.
It wasn't a serum.
It was alive.
SILASThey didn't give us powers.
They planted something.
It grows. It listens.
SILASAnd it is not ours.
The biology wasn't a gift.
It was a seed.
Foundry Global.
The company that sells tomorrow.
It also owns the ship.
KELLHow many are awake?
OKAFORAll of them, Director.
They've been awake for a year.
Foundry calls them specimens.
They are not asleep.
They are waiting.
Paragon smiles because he has to.
HANDLERSmile, Grant.
The vote is Tuesday.
The Ascension Act: seed screening for every first responder in America.
Two million people. Ninety days.
Foundry wrote it. Paragon sells it.
Nobody asked who owns the seed.
KELLYou think I'm the villain.
KELLI'm the only one who's been
holding the door shut.
Reyes has been followed for a week.
He wasn't followed here. He was brought.
KELLThat thing isn't an invasion.
It's a warning.
From the ones who were here first.
Two kinds of visitors.
One that knocks. One that never left.
They have no ships.
They have memory.
MARAWe have watched the Pale
seed twelve worlds.
MARAEvery one of them
called it progress.
They call themselves Residents.
They have called this planet home for four thousand years.
The seed does not make gods.
MARAIt makes doors.
When enough are open,
they come through.
MARAAnd they wear you.
The Ascension was never ours.
The Act's fine print: two million seeds in ninety days.
Threshold reached on day sixty-one.
Okafor did the math.
Then she did the wrong thing,
which was the right thing.
HOLLOWDoctor.
You were told to stop looking.
Some of the first generation didn't die.
They just stopped being home.
HALESubject Nine is the only door
we can't open.
Bring him to the tower.
PARAGONAlive?
HALEBring him.
At 11:52 PM, every hero in the city
looked up at the same moment.
Same direction. Same sky.

CHAPTER 3
ASCENSION
Every empire has a launch date.
Tuesday. 4:00 PM.
The Senate votes on the Ascension Act.
Foundry expects it to pass.
Foundry wrote the count.
KELLThe seeds sync through the Beacon on the tower.
Cut it, and the doors close.
MARACutting isn't enough.
The seeds are inside them.
Something has to burn.
SILASI can burn one.
KELLThrough the Beacon, you'd be
burning through all of them.
SILASI was already dead in that chair.
This is paperwork.
Nobody argued.
That was the worst part.
Paragon came alone.
Foundry told him it would be easy.
PARAGONDon't make me do this, Nine.
SILASYou're not doing anything, Grant.
It is.
The burn takes seconds.
It costs years.
For the first time since he was twenty-two,
Grant Ellery heard silence.
PARAGON...it's quiet.
HALEOf course I know what the seed is, Doctor.
HALEDo you know what they offered?
When they come through,
someone has to hold the leash.
OKAFORThey don't offer, Victor.
They farm.
Every cover-up has a man
who thinks he's the exception.
It passed, 71 to 29.
The doors opened at 4:15 PM.
Ten thousand heroes stopped moving.
Then they all turned toward the tower.
The first generation finally fought for something.
PARAGONHold on. Both of you.
I'm not great at gentle.
VEILGo! I've been dying since '09.
I'm good at it.
Veil held the stairs for four minutes.
It was enough.
HALEYou can't stop a harvest
by killing the farmer.
HOLLOWWE HAVE WAITED.
YOU ARE READY.
Kell didn't answer him.
Kell had answered him in 1998,
in a room with no windows.
Two rounds. The exception fell.
One man. Ten thousand doors.
Every one of them slammed shut.
SILASTell them what it was, Reyes.
All of it.
Even the parts they'll hate.
There wasn't a body.
There was a shape, burned into the alloy.
It was standing up.
MARAThey will try again.
They always try again.
MARABut now you know what you are.
The powers stayed. The doors didn't.
For the first time, the heroes were only theirs.
Reyes released everything. Forty-one thousand files.
Names, dates, crash sites, the serum, the seed.
It took the world four days to believe it,
and one to stop pretending it hadn't.
Okafor testified for nine hours. Kell for six.
Grant Ellery for two, and only one thing mattered:
GRANTIt was never a gift.
It was a lease.
We just paid it off.
The truth is out there.
Now it's in here.
And it didn't kill him. Not this time.
Roswell wasn't the end.
Neither was this.
