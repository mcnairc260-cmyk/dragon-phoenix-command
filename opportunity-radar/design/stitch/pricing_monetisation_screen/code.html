Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later? expand_more
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, the change is immediate and the price will be prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
What counts as a "scan"? expand_more
A scan is a single AI-powered request to analyze a specific keyword, competitor, or social media niche. The Explorer plan limits this to ensure server stability for our premium users.
Do you offer a refund policy? expand_more
We offer a 14-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee for all new Innovator and Founder subscriptions. We want you to be 100% satisfied with your data quality.
