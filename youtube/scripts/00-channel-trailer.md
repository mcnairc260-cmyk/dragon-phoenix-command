# CHANNEL TRAILER — "WELCOME TO THE COMMAND CENTER"

**Runtime:** ~90 seconds
**Purpose:** Convert a cold visitor into a subscriber by naming their exact frustration, reframing it, and showing them the world (brand, lore, promise).
**Voice:** Calm, precise, cinematic. No shouting. Confidence, not hype.
**Music:** Dark ambient pulse building to a restrained, powerful crescendo. Never EDM-drop energy.

## Production notes

- **Total VO word count:** ~215 words — comfortable at 90s with cinematic pauses.
- **Beats 1–4 are the retention gate:** first 10 seconds must feel personal, not epic. Save the spectacle for beat 8 onward.
- **Color:** Void Black base; cyan for the "machine/understanding" visuals; ember/gold reserved for Dragon/Phoenix/brand moments.
- **Type:** Display sans for title cards; monospace HUD labels (e.g., `ATTENTION SYSTEM // ONLINE`) as background texture.
- **Sound design:** ember crackle motif recurs at each section transition — the channel's audio signature.
- **End screen:** link "Start Here" playlist (videos 1–3).
