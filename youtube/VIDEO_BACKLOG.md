## Phase 1 — The Spine (videos 1–12)

**1. 🔭 Why Smart People Procrastinate**
- Hook: "Procrastination isn't a discipline problem. It's an emotion-regulation problem — and smart people are often *better* at it."
- Mechanism: procrastination as short-term mood repair; task aversion; the amygdala–prefrontal tug; why intelligence adds better excuses.
- System: the 10-Minute Contract + task de-uglification (shrink, clarify, start signal).
- Thumbnail: chess king lying on its side, ember glow. Text: "SMART = WORSE?"

**2. 🔭 Motivation Is a Lie (Here's What Actually Works)**
- Hook: "You don't have a motivation problem. You have a prediction problem."
- Mechanism: dopamine as reward *prediction*, not pleasure; why anticipation beats reward; why motivation follows action.
- System: the Ignition Protocol — lower activation energy, act first, let motivation catch up. Systems before willpower (Law 2).
- Thumbnail: dead match vs. glowing system diagram. Text: "MOTIVATION IS A LIE"

**3. 🔭 Your Focus Isn't Broken. It's Being Farmed.**
- Hook: "Ten thousand engineers went to work today to take what you're about to try to protect."
- Mechanism: the attention economy (The Static); variable reward schedules; attention residue; task-switching costs.
- System: the Fortress Hour — one engineered distraction-free block; environment design over resistance.
- Thumbnail: eye with circuit lines harvesting toward phones. Text: "FARMED"

**4. 🔭 How Habits Are Actually Built (Not the 21-Day Myth)**
- Hook: "The 21-day habit rule came from a plastic surgeon's observation in the 1960s — and it's wrong."
- Mechanism: cue-routine-reward, context-dependent automaticity, realistic timelines (weeks–months), identity-based habits.
- System: the Minimum Viable Habit — 2-minute version anchored to an existing cue.
- Thumbnail: "21 DAYS" crossed out in fire. Text: "THE REAL NUMBER"

**5. 🔭 ADHD: What It Actually Is (From Someone Who Lives It)**
- Hook: "It's not a deficit of attention. It's a difference in how attention is *paid*."
- Mechanism: executive function, interest-based nervous system, hyperfocus paradox, dopamine regulation differences. Educational + lived experience; explicit not-medical-advice disclaimer.
- System: externalize everything — the Second Brain starter (capture, visible cues, body doubling).
- Thumbnail: brain as switchboard, some lines blazing, some dark. Text: "NOT A DEFICIT"

**6. 🔨 Build a Day That Doesn't Need Willpower**
- Hook: "By 10am you've already made 100 decisions. Here's how to make the day run itself."
- Mechanism: decision fatigue, defaults, friction design.
- System: the Default Day template — pre-decided anchors, if-then plans (implementation intentions).
- Thumbnail: cockpit checklist glowing. Text: "ZERO WILLPOWER"

**7. 🔭 Why Confidence Doesn't Come From Believing in Yourself**
- Hook: "Affirmations don't build confidence. Evidence does."
- Mechanism: self-efficacy (Bandura), competence loops, why fake confidence collapses under pressure.
- System: the Evidence Ledger — small wins logged as proof; scoped challenges slightly beyond comfort.
- Thumbnail: mirror vs. logbook. Text: "PROOF > BELIEF"

**8. ⚙️ AI Won't Replace You. But It Will Expose You.**
- Hook: "AI doesn't make thinking optional. It makes *clear thinking* the only thing left that's scarce."
- Mechanism: what LLMs actually do; amplification vs. dependency (The Mimic); why weak questions get weak answers.
- System: the Operator's Protocol — think first, draft with AI, verify, decide yourself. Human keeps the chair.
- Thumbnail: human silhouette amplified by light vs. hollowed by it. Text: "EXPOSED"

**9. 🔭 The Real Reason You Quit Things**
- Hook: "You don't quit because you're lazy. You quit at a very predictable spot — and you can see it coming."
- Mechanism: the motivation dip / valley of disappointment; goal-gradient effect; expectation vs. reality curves.
- System: the Dip Map — pre-planning for week 3; Phoenix protocol for restarting without shame.
- Thumbnail: progress curve with a marked cliff. Text: "YOU QUIT *HERE*"

**10. 🔭 Learning How to Learn: The Skill School Never Taught**
- Hook: "You spent 12+ years in school and nobody taught you the one skill it was all built on."
- Mechanism: retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving, desirable difficulty, illusion of fluency (why rereading feels good and does little).
- System: the Ascension Study Loop — test-first learning with spaced review.
- Thumbnail: highlighter snapped in half, ember glow. Text: "SCHOOL LIED"

**11. 🔨 The Second Brain: Stop Trusting Your Memory**
- Hook: "Your brain is for having ideas, not holding them."
- Mechanism: working memory limits, cognitive offloading, the Zeigarnik effect (open loops drain attention).
- System: a minimal capture→organize→retrieve pipeline (tool-agnostic).
- Thumbnail: overflowing brain → clean console. Text: "STOP REMEMBERING"

**12. 📉 What 90 Days of Building This Channel Taught Me (Flight Data #1)**
- Hook: "I teach systems. Here's exactly where mine failed."
- Mechanism: honest post-mortem — analytics, failures, lessons; failures become documentation (Art. XI).
- System: the Flight Data template viewers can use for their own projects.
- Thumbnail: real analytics screenshot, one metric circled in ember. Text: "IT FAILED"
