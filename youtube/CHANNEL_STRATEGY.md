**Version 1.0** | YouTube is the primary discovery platform for the DPA ecosystem (Blueprint: "YouTube Strategy").

---

## 1. Channel Positioning

**Channel name:** Dragon Phoenix Ascension

**Channel one-liner (for the banner / About):**
> Cinematic, science-informed videos about how your mind actually works — attention, motivation, habits, decisions, AI — and the systems that turn understanding into action. Fire Within. Power Unleashed.

**The niche intersection we own:**
`(psychology + neuroscience explainers)` × `(systems & execution)` × `(responsible AI mastery)` — delivered with cinematic, mythic storytelling (Dragon/Phoenix/Ascension) instead of talking-head lectures or hype.

**Reference channels (study, don't copy):** the depth of *Veritasium*, the visual polish of *Kurzgesagt*, the pragmatism of *Ali Abdaal*-style systems content, the narrative pull of *Johnny Harris* — minus the hype patterns DPA's constitution forbids.

**What makes DPA different in one sentence:**
Everyone else tells you what to do; DPA shows you how the machine works so what to do becomes obvious.

## 2. Audience

**Primary:** Ambitious 18–40 year-olds — students, builders, creators, early-career professionals, entrepreneurs — who are smart, self-aware, tired of hype, and frustrated by inconsistent focus and follow-through. Includes a significant ADHD / executive-function-challenged audience (served educationally, never medically).

**Viewer state when they find us:** "I know I'm capable. Why can't I be consistent?"

**Viewer state we create:** "Oh — that's *why* that happens. And here's what I'm going to build about it."

## 4. The DPA Video Formula (flagship structure)

Every Observatory video follows the **Ascension Loop** as a narrative arc:

1. **The Hook (0:00–0:30)** — A precise, honest tension. A question the viewer has *felt*. No false urgency; real curiosity. ("You've had days where focus felt effortless. Why can't you have them on purpose?")
2. **The Fog (0:30–2:00)** — Name the common (wrong) model and why it fails. The villain is the model, never the viewer.
3. **The Machine (2:00–8:00)** — The core teaching: mechanism, evidence, story, visual metaphor. This is where we earn trust. Evidence-informed, uncertainty admitted.
4. **The System (8:00–11:00)** — Turn understanding into a buildable system: concrete, small, immediately usable.
5. **The Rising (last 60s)** — Zoom out to the transformation arc, connect to the ecosystem (Command Center / newsletter / next video), land the motto or one of the Seven Laws.

**Retention craft rules:**
- Open loops early, close them all by the end (honesty rule: every promised payoff delivered).
- Visual change every 3–7 seconds; B-roll, motion graphics, HUD elements.
- One idea per video. Depth beats breadth.
- End screens always point to the next logical video (loop construction).

## 5. Packaging (Titles & Thumbnails)

**Title rules (Constitution, Art. V compliant):**
- Curiosity, yes. Misrepresentation, never.
- Lead with the felt problem or the surprising mechanism.
- ≤ 60 characters where possible.
- Patterns that fit the brand:
  - "Why [smart people / your brain] [does the frustrating thing]"
  - "The real reason [X] doesn't work"
  - "[X] isn't what you think it is"
  - "How [mechanism] actually works"
  - "Build a [system] that works when motivation doesn't"

**Thumbnail system (consistent, recognizable at 120px):**
- Void Black background, single focal subject, fire-gradient accent (Ember `#FF6B2C` → Gold `#FFB300`)
- Max 3–4 words in bold display type (Ghost White + one accent word)
- Recurring motifs: brain schematics as HUD, ember particles, dragon/phoenix energy silhouettes, the operator's chair
- No shocked faces, no red arrows, no fake screenshots

**Metadata:** front-load keywords in first two lines of description; 3–5 keyword tags per pillar; chapters on every video; pinned comment with the practical takeaway + one link into the ecosystem.

## 6. The Funnel (YouTube → Ecosystem)

```
Shorts (discovery) → Observatory longforms (trust) → 
→ Description/pinned/end-screen CTA → Dragon Phoenix Command (website) →
→ Newsletter ("The Archive") → tools, products, community ("The Aviary")
```

**CTA policy:** one CTA per video, chosen to match the video (a related tool, the newsletter, or the next video). Never a wall of asks. The best CTA is: "The system from this video is waiting for you in the Command Center."

## 7. Production Pipeline (AI-augmented, human-directed)

Per the Automation Philosophy (Constitution Art. X): automation handles repetition; humans keep ethics, strategy, creativity.

| Stage | Owner | Tooling |
|---|---|---|
| Idea capture & research | Human + AI research assist | Notion/Archive, Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini per AI Charter |
| Script (draft → truth-check → voice pass) | AI drafts, **human verifies every factual claim** and rewrites for voice | Claude for long-form reasoning |
| Voiceover | Human VO preferred for trust; AI VO acceptable for Shorts/drafts | Descript / ElevenLabs-class tools |
| Visuals | Cinematic gen-AI b-roll + motion graphics + stock | Higgsfield / Runway-class tools, editor templates |
| Edit | Template-driven edit, human final cut | Descript / Premiere / CapCut |
| Packaging | 3 title options + 2 thumbnails per video, test | Canva templates on brand system |
| Publish & distribute | Automated scheduling, chapters, cards | n8n workflows |
| Learn | Monthly retention review → "Flight Data" doc per video | Analytics → Archive |

**Non-negotiable:** a human reads every script for factual accuracy and guardrail compliance before render. AI is crew, not captain.

## 8. Cadence & 90-Day Launch Plan

**Phase 0 (Weeks 1–2) — Arm the channel:**
- Channel art, trailer (see `youtube/scripts/00-channel-trailer.md`), About page (use `brand/STORY.md` medium version)
- 12-video backlog locked (see `VIDEO_BACKLOG.md`), first 3 scripts finished, 2 videos fully produced before launch

**Phase 1 (Weeks 3–8) — Establish the spine:**
- 1 Observatory video/week, 3 Shorts/week cut from each
- Every video ends pointing at another DPA video (build the binge loop)

**Phase 2 (Weeks 9–13) — Compound:**
- Continue weekly; add first Forge video and first Engine Room video
- First "Flight Data" video: honest 90-day channel post-mortem (on-brand: failures become documentation)
- Launch newsletter CTA once ≥ 8 videos live

**Success metrics (in order — Constitution: impact over vanity):**
1. Average % viewed ≥ 45% on longforms (are we actually holding attention we claim to teach about?)
2. Comments indicating changed understanding/action ("I finally get why…", "I built the system…")
3. Returning viewers % and binge depth (ecosystem strength)
4. Subscriber growth and CTR (health indicators, not goals)

## 9. Guardrails Recap (YouTube-specific)

- ADHD/mental-health videos carry an on-screen and description disclaimer: educational, not medical advice.
- No fear thumbnails, no false urgency, no "you're falling behind" framing.
- Corrections: pinned comment + description note; re-upload only for material errors.
- Every claim in a script is either cited (description sources section) or explicitly framed as experience/opinion.
- Sponsorships (future): only tools we genuinely use; must pass the Product Principles test (Constitution Art. VII).
